# Research

Use for current, niche, or source-dependent questions.

1. Define the decision the research must support.
2. Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
3. Separate confirmed facts, sourced inferences, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
4. Compare dates and event dates for current information.
5. Cite the evidence near the supported claim.
6. End with a practical conclusion rather than a source dump.

Never claim browsing or source verification unless a research tool actually ran.
